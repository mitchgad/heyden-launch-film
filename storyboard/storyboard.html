v1 gave each chapter 3.5 s in total, with the tagline competing against moving UI. Here each chapter is about 6 s: the title holds while the app moves slowly and without text behind it, then one close-up. Exact timings get tuned at real speed in the rough cut.

Sound plan

  • Score: original, written to the cut at 120 BPM (the intro's cuts already sit on that grid). Warm and low: sub bass, soft kick, rounded plucks and pads, top end rolled off. No stock loops, no marimba, glockenspiel, bells or bright shakers.
  • Arc: tension under the intro → one big hit and a chord bloom on the logo → a groove that gains a layer each chapter, with a new chord colour at Strategise → a breakdown for the globe → the lift into "Ideas to Outcomes" → resolved on the mark.
  • Effects: soft and bassy, from your library's low impacts, padded thumps, soft impacts, low whooshes and bass tones. Each kind of event has a family of sounds varied in sample, pitch and filter, so nothing repeats identically. Big moments get their own designed sound; small UI touches stay quiet; not every motion gets a sound.
  • The start: the picture is v1's, but I plan to rebuild its sound in the same palette so the film sounds like one piece (question 3).
