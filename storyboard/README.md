# Storyboard

The planning stage for the product section, before the final was built. Start with `heyden-v3-storyboard.pdf`
(10 pages); the same pages are in `pages/` as PNGs.

- `platform/`: the four designed pages of the Heyden platform at 3360×2160, in their finished states.
  - `01-projects.png`: Collaborate
  - `02-quick-assessment.png`: Innovate
  - `03-strategy-hub.png`: Strategise
  - `04-commons-ask-heyden.png`: Solve
- `frames/`: the 18 storyboard panels at 1920×1080, each rendered from the film's engine at the time in its file name,
  without motion blur.
- `storyboard.html`: the source of the PDF.

The storyboard was made for a 40-second cut. The final film runs 50 seconds, with more time on each product scene, a
different line over the globe ("A Global Innovation Hub for Emergency Services.") and a new soundtrack. The last page
lists the questions asked during planning.
