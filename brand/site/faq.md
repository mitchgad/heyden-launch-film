FAQ

The answers to our most commonly asked questions live here.

Data Sovereignty

Where is agency data hosted and who controls it?

Heyden customers retain ownership of their own data. All data is stored in your operating location.

Cybersecurity

How does Heyden protect sensitive operational data?

Heyden has been designed to manage sensitve government procurement data for agencies across the globe. We have a compliance framework that tracks SOC 2 Type II, ISO 28001 2022 and ISM IRAP controls.

Multilingual

Which languages does Heyden support?

Heyden currently supports English, French and Spanish with the option to add more languages at customer request.

Heyden Roadmap

What if I need a feature that is not currently supported?

Heyden was build on customer needs and will continue to be built on customer needs. If you have a feature request, please contact us and we will consider it for the next release.

Integrations

Can Heyden support integrations into existing systems?

we have the capacity to support API and MCP integration on an as needed basis. Please contact us to discuss your requirements.
